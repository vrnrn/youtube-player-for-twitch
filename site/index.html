About the extension

YouTube Player for Twitch adds a YouTube player to Twitch channel pages. It can look for live streams on the channel’s linked YouTube page, search by channel name when needed, and remember recent streams and playback preferences in Chrome.

The extension is developed and maintained by Varun Rana. This site provides app information and the extension’s Privacy Policy.
